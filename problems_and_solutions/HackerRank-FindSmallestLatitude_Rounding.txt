Question ->
Query the smallest Northern Latitude (LAT_N) from STATION that is greater than x. Round your answer to  decimal places.


Table -> Station
ID: Number
CITY: varchar2(21)
STATE: varchar2(2)
LAT_N: number
LONG_W: Number

################################################################################

SOLUTION ->
SELECT
round(LAT_N,4)
FROM STATION
where LAT_N > 38.7780
ORDER by LAT_N ASC
LIMIT 1
;
