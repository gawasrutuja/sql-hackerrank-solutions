Question ->
Query the Western Longitude (LONG_W)where the smallest Northern Latitude (LAT_N) in STATION is greater than x. Round your answer to 4 decimal places.



Table -> Station
ID: Number
CITY: varchar2(21)
STATE: varchar2(2)
LAT_N: number
LONG_W: Number

################################################################################

SOLUTION ->
SELECT
round(LONG_W,4)
FROM STATION
WHERE LAT_N > 38.7780
ORDER BY LAT_N ASC
LIMIT 1
;
